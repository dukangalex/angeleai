FEATURESEvery kernel capability, within reach

Built on the Mihomo core. Everything the kernel can do is a tappable option in the UI, each with a sourced explanation — nothing hidden, nothing padded with jargon.

◆True chained proxy

A complete entry-to-exit chain: if the entry dies, the whole chain stops — it never silently falls back to a direct connection. Chain health is visible on the config card, with the cause and the fix spelled out.

◆All capabilities explicit

Modes, routing, DNS, speed tests — every behaviour has exactly one authoritative path, no conflicts, no duplicates. In rule mode, China traffic goes direct and everything else goes through the proxy. That line does not move.

◆Zero-setup start

Install, paste or scan your subscription, and it just runs: a working node is picked automatically, rules and China adaptation are in place by default, and no technical decision is ever pushed onto you.

◆Twelve constellation themes

The “Sea of Stars” theme family: one theme per constellation, drawn from its element and imagery. Sagittarius · Indigo Night Arrow is the default; all twelve are built in.

◆Honest status

Linked speed tests measure without silently re-picking your node. The indicator light shares the same source as the measurement: green when usable, red when not, grey when untested.

◆Built to run for years

Effort goes into the fundamentals: stable connections, reliable subscription updates, accurate tests — verified against memory and battery, not feature lists.

PRIVACYPrivacy is the premise, not an option

  • Local firstGeo lookups run entirely on-device with zero outbound queries. Data that can stay on your machine does.
  • Redacted by defaultLogs, diagnostics and exports are scrubbed before they are written — no subscription links or credentials in plain text.
  • Minimal permissionsNo unnecessary permissions, no AI layer, and your diagnostics are never sent to a third party.
